02 ABOUT ME

Designing the systems fintech trust runs on

I'm a Systems & DevOps Engineer who got pulled into backend work through a simple question: how does money move through software without ever losing a cent along the way? That question is now the thread through everything I build — double-entry ledgers, async payment callbacks, and infrastructure that doesn't flinch under load.

My stack centers on Python (FastAPI, Asyncio), PostgreSQL, and container-native infrastructure — but the deeper skill is architectural judgment: knowing when to trade consistency for availability, how to gate a deploy pipeline against CVEs before they ship, and how to keep a multi-tenant system fast without leaking one client's data into another's.

Recently that's meant shipping multi-tenant SaaS platforms, M-Pesa & SMS integrations, and Kubernetes-orchestrated banking systems — the unglamorous, high-stakes work of financial engineering. I care about code that's still easy to read a year from now, and infrastructure that lets a team sleep through a deploy.
